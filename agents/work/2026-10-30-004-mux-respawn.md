## Summary

`respawn` starts a new process in an existing pane with a fresh screen, at the old pane's size. It keeps the pane's ID and its place in its window, hangs up a live process, and moves attached clients to the new screen. A command that fails to start leaves the pane as it was. The plan's goal is met.
