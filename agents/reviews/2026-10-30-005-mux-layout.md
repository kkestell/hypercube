# Review of mux layout and resizing

## Scope and coverage

I reviewed the staged changes for task 5 of milestone 1 in `agents/mux-design.md`, against `agents/plans/2026-10-30-005-mux-layout.md` and the work log `agents/work/2026-10-30-005-mux-layout.md`. That covers `layout` in `mux/internal/server/layout.go`, `layoutSession`, `stream`, `wake`, and `drawFrame` in `attach.go`, the calls from `spawn`, `kill`, and `respawn` in `server.go`, the early return in `pane.resize`, and the new and changed tests in `mux/cmd/mux/main_test.go`. I traced pane sizes through attach, resize, spawn, kill, respawn, and detach; attachments whose session a kill ends; and the frame's offsets, divider, and cursor.

I checked each deviation in the work log. Signaling from `layoutSession` removes repeated signals. Removing `removePane` drops a function that only called `hangUp`. The registration check before routing input is needed: input decoded after a kill ends the session would otherwise index an empty window list and crash the server. The empty layout for a terminal with no columns or under four rows keeps `layoutSession` from giving a pane a zero size, which would panic; shrinking a terminal that far is uncommon but reachable. The exact `size=` match in `checkSize` fixes a real false match.

Gaps:

- Nothing was checked by hand in a live terminal; the frame layout was checked through the tests.

## Verdict

The change meets the plan and the design's task 5 check. Panes are laid out at 40% and stacked, every window in the session follows the client's size, detaching returns panes to 80 by 24, panes that do not fit are skipped, input and the cursor follow the main pane, and the reserved bar and title rows leave task 6 free to draw without resizing. Nothing needs fixing.
